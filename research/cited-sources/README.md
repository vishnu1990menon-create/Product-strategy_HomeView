## 3. Findings to carry back into the page

1. **Apple and Tesla headline figures are confirmed** against the primary filings (table above).
2. **AirPods 5 "noise cancellation reaching the entry tier" is not confirmed.** The Hindu says AirPods 5 have an open-ear design, up to 1.5x more ANC than the previous generation, Personalised Spatial Audio with head tracking and Siri AI with Live Translation. It prices in rupees only, with a higher variant adding wireless charging case, force sensor and longer battery. The AirPods 5 entry on the page's feature rail ("noise cancellation reaches the entry tier") rests on that claim; a search snippet from a low-quality site gave different US pricing, so treat any entry-tier or $129 claim as unverified until Apple's announcement is checked.
3. **Watch Series 12:** The Hindu describes the Series 12 as "re-designed with more capable optical and electrical sensors" with a new Health Sensing System on the S11 chip. The case study calls the release "incremental rather than a redesign". The sensor claim is consistent with "upgraded heart-rate sensing"; the "incremental" label is an editorial judgment.
4. **IDC wording:** see the IDC row above. The page no longer charts these numbers.
